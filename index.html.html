How PageTurn works

Three easy steps to keep good books moving.

01

Discover a book

Explore books by title, genre or locality. See approximate locations, never private home addresses.

02

Connect & borrow

Request a book, coordinate delivery through private chat and pay a refundable 50% caution deposit.

03

Read, return, repeat

Enjoy your read, arrange the return, and track your refund transparently once the lender confirms receipt.
