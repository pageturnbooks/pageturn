How PageTurn works

Three easy steps to keep good books moving.

01

Discover a book

Explore books by title, genre or locality. See approximate locations, never private home addresses.

02

Connect & borrow

Request a book, pay a refundable 50% caution deposit plus a ₹50 issue fee, and coordinate through private chat. Borrower and lender self-arrange delivery using a local Porter or courier service; courier charges are separate.

03

Read, return, repeat

Return the book within 3 months of issuance. Arrange return shipping yourself through a local Porter or courier service. After the lender confirms satisfactory return, PageTurn initiates the caution-deposit refund within seven calendar days.

ReIssue Policy

Need more reading time? Every book is issued for 3 months. One extension of up to 3 additional months may be requested before the due date and requires the book owner's approval. For an approved reissue, the borrower pays an additional refundable caution deposit of 50% of the agreed book value plus a new ₹50 issue fee (less eligible points redemption on the fee). The original deposit remains held as security: together the two deposits equal 100% of the agreed book value. Both caution deposits are refundable after satisfactory return, subject to the documented dispute process. Maximum total borrowing period: 6 months; no further extension. If the owner declines reissue, the book must be returned by its existing due date. Courier charges remain separate and self-arranged.
